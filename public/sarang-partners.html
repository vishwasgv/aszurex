Sarang Business OS Lite

Partner Program Terms

Last updated: September 2026. These terms apply to referral partners who introduce customers to Sarang Business OS Lite.

1. What the partner program is

The Sarang partner program lets accountants, IT dealers, consultants and other trusted people introduce shops and businesses to Sarang and earn a referral commission on the license fee we receive. It is a referral arrangement only. A partner is an independent person or business, not our employee, agent or joint venturer.

2. How you join

Write to contact@aszurex.com with your name, business name, city, phone number and, for India, your PAN (and GSTIN if you have one). We review each request by hand and may decline it without giving a reason. If we accept you, we send you a unique partner code and a partner link.

3. How a referral is counted

A sale counts as yours when the customer pays for a Sarang license using your partner code or partner link, and the payment is completed and cleared. Only AszureX issues license keys, always directly to the customer through our own systems. You cannot issue keys, collect license payments, change prices, give discounts or make promises on our behalf.

4. Commission

The commission is 20% of the license fee we actually receive for the customer's first paid license year, excluding any tax and payment-provider charges we do not keep. Extra seats bought at the same time count as part of that first year's fee. Renewals and later years do not earn commission unless we tell you so in writing. We total commission each month and pay it within 30 days after the month ends, for sales whose payment cleared. License payments are non-refundable, so commission is not clawed back; if we refund a payment because the law requires it or because of our own error, the commission on that sale is cancelled.

5. Taxes on commission

Commission is paid after any deduction of tax at source that the law requires us to make, and we will give you the details on request. You are responsible for your own income tax, and for GST or any other tax on your commission if you are required to register or charge it. If you are GST-registered, send us your GSTIN and a valid tax invoice for the commission.

6. What partners must not do

  • Make false or misleading claims about Sarang, including claims about tax, legal compliance, security or results. Sarang helps prepare figures and files; it is not tax advice and does not replace an accountant.
  • Sell, share or install keys that did not come from us, or help anyone get around the license checks.
  • Use our name, logo or brand in a way that suggests you are us, or register look-alike names or domains.
  • Send unsolicited bulk messages or collect customers' personal data without their consent.
  • Use paid ads on our brand name or on the terms "Sarang" plus "license key" without our written permission.

7. Ending the arrangement

Either side may end the partner arrangement at any time by writing to the other. We may end it at once, withhold unpaid commission on sales made in breach, and revoke any key sold in breach of these terms, if you break section 6. Commission already earned and cleared, on sales not made in breach, remains payable.

8. Changes, law and disputes

We may change the commission rate or these terms for future sales by giving you 30 days' notice by email. These terms are governed by the laws of India, and section 17 of the Sarang Terms & Conditions (resolving disagreements) applies to them. In any conflict between these partner terms and the Sarang Terms & Conditions about licenses, the Sarang Terms & Conditions apply.
